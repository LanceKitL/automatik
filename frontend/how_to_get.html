How to Get an Account

Automatik · User Access Guide

Access to the system is managed by the company. User accounts are created based on your role and responsibilities. Self-registration is not available. All credentials are issued through official company channels by an authorized Administrator.

Account types

Agent

Internal
  • 1Created by an Administrator via the Admin Panel.
  • 2A verification email is sent to the registered address.
  • 3Activate by completing email verification.
  • 4For authorized company personnel only.

Customer

External
  • 1Created by an Administrator or Agent during vehicle purchase.
  • 2No self-registration is available.
  • 3A temporary password is issued upon account creation.
  • 4Password change may be required on first login.

Finance Staff

Internal
  • 1Created exclusively by an Administrator.
  • 2Credentials are issued through company procedures.
  • 3Access limited to authorized finance personnel.
  • 4For internal company use only.

Service Staff

Internal
  • 1Created exclusively by an Administrator.
  • 2Credentials are issued through company procedures.
  • 3Access limited to authorized service personnel.
  • 4For internal company use only.

Credential delivery summary

Credential delivery by role

Role Created by How credentials are received
Agent Administrator Email verification link
Customer Administrator or Agent Temporary password
Finance Staff Administrator Company-issued credentials
Service Staff Administrator Company-issued credentials

Need an account? Contact your company administrator or authorized representative. Only authorized personnel can create user accounts in this system. Do not share credentials with others.
