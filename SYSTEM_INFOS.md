# Scope & Delimitations

**Scope and Delimitations** 

This study focuses on the design and development of the **Car Dealership Management System** for Dealership Companies. The system aims to modernize the traditional dealership operations by using **Role Based Access** for each user. The system provides a portal where agents can *self-assign* inquiries, create their own to-do list and track their sales and commissions. It allows admins to manage inventory, service   
slots, agents, customers, inquiries, documents  and sale status efficiently. It also helps customers track their amortization, documents, process warranty claims and interact with AI-Chatbot for faster customer support. 

The system includes the following features:

* User authentication and account management  
* Inventory Management  
* Agent Performance Monitoring  
* AI-Powered Chatbot  
* Document Generation  
* Customer Portal  
* Real-time Email and In-app Notifications  
* Amortization Calculator  
* Finance and Insurance Portal  
* Warranty and Service Portal

The study does not integrate with third party banks, payment gateways and LTO API. The system can only be accessed by authorized users within the institution's network. Tracking of orders, procurement, human resource management are declared out of scope in this project.

Furthermore, the study focuses on **Project Management** and functionality testing only. Large-scale deployment and long-term maintenance are not covered in this research.
